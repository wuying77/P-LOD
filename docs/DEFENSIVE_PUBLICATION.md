# Defensive Publication & Public Commons Notice

**P-LOD** (Progressive Strong-Entanglement Point Array with Weak-Entanglement Emergence Encoding) is published as **prior art** and as a **public technical asset** for humanity.

## Intent

This repository, its specifications, examples, codecs, diagrams, and accompanying documentation are intentionally and permanently placed in the public technical record so that:

1. **No commercial entity, intermediary, or patent aggregator** can credibly claim exclusive ownership over the core ideas, data-frame layouts, progressive level scheme, strong/weak entanglement encoding model, or reference implementations disclosed here.
2. **Any later attempt to patent, privatize, or monopoly-license** substantially the same technical subject matter is opposed by this **timestamped public disclosure** (Git history, public README, Specification v1.0, examples, and related materials).
3. **Developers worldwide** may implement, extend, ship, and commercialize products *using* P-LOD under the MIT License **without** paying a toll to a private gatekeeper for the core protocol itself.

## What this does *not* mean

- It does **not** ban commercial products that *implement* P-LOD. MIT allows commercial use.
- It does **not** transfer ownership of third-party trademarks, brand assets, or unrelated patents.
- It does **not** replace formal legal advice in any jurisdiction.

## What it does mean

P-LOD’s architecture, wire formats, progressive skeleton model, and open reference code are **defensively published**: the goal is freedom to build, not a private patent moat.

If a party files for exclusive rights over the same disclosed methods after this publication, this repository and its commit history are intended to serve as **citable prior art**.
